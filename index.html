Sheet A2

How a project actually runs

01

Quote call

Twenty minutes with the engineer who'd actually build your site — not a salesperson. You describe what you need, and get an honest read on what it takes and roughly what it costs.

02

The blueprint

A short written plan lands in your inbox: pages, features, a timeline, and a fixed price. Nothing starts until you've said yes to that document.

03

Build

We build against a private preview link you can open anytime — no waiting for a "big reveal." Short weekly check-ins keep it moving in the right direction.

04

Launch

We move it onto your domain, test it on real devices, and hand you the login details and a short doc on how to update things yourself.

05

Care, if you want it

Optional monthly support once you're live — someone on call for fixes, edits, and keeping things running.
